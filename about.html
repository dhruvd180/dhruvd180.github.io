Our Story

Born from Necessity

BigCatID was founded when I visited the Wild Source's Njozi Camp in the Northern Serengeti, where biologists were spend huge amounts of time manually ID'ing cheetahs, leopards, and lions.

I came up with a simple goal: utilize AI image recognition technology to identify big cats based on their spot, rosette, or under-eye patterns.
